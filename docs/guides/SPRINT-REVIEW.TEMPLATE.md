## Sprint Review | Template
## Guiding Questions | TEMPLATE

### Scope and Depth

> Is the sprint idea targeting an important, impactful research direction?
> 

> How comprehensive is the research question in addressing the core issue? Does it capture all relevant dimensions of the problem?
> 

> Are there any critical aspects of the problem that the current idea fails to explore?
> 

### Methodological Rigor

> How certain can we be of the conclusion? Are there situations where the conclusion isn’t necessarily true with the given results?
> 

> Are there potential confounding variables?
> 

> Are the results statistically significant?
> 

> How large is the sample size? How well does the sample represent the underlying distribution?
> 

### Place relative to Literature

> How well does the idea integrate and build upon existing literature and research in this field?
> 

> What is the gap between the sprint submission and the closest, most similar paper?
> 

### Extending the Idea

> How could the idea be extended or generalized?
> 

> Are there any potential applications or practical implications of the idea that haven't been explored?
