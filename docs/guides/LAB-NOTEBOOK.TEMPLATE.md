## Lab Notebook Template

> #### What is a Lab notebook?
> A lab notebook is a record of your research process, including experiments or other work conducted. It can describe your experimental methods, observations you make during your research, and the reasoning behind any decisions you take. Each lab notebook entry should be written immediately after the activity or work was performed.
> 
> Successfully capturing this information is critical to researchers - both for the writing process and to be able to track your work in future. It also helps protect your ideas and is often used when applying for patents.
