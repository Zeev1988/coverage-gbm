## The Main Result Figure
#### Why Start Early?

Defining this figure now will be instrumentally useful in

- Verifying that the experiments you have planned will actually capture the data required to make meaningful conclusions
- Writing the first draft of your work

---

Reflect on the following:

- What are the results we want to highlight in this figure?
- What is the story that this figure is telling? In other words, what is the takeaway for a reader?

Even if you don’t have all of your results yet, put together a figure that *would* show what you aim to demonstrate

Be specific in the data which are required to create that plot. This will help you think through whether the experiments you are currently doing will enable you to get to this stage.

For these figures, it is also relevant to begin thinking about *the format of the data which would be easiest to work with;* keep this in mind when choosing how to save and store your experimental data.

Keep in mind that the plot should clearly visualize *uncertainty* in the results. Consider the different steps in which error or variance may leak into the data, and verify that this uncertainty can be quantified using the recorded information.
