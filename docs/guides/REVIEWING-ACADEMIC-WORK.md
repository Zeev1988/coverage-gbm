## Reviewing Academic Works
One way to understand research rigor is by examining existing papers and their reviews. For example, the International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) has their review process publicly available on openreview.net. You can search for ICLR papers and their reviews using `ICLR 2024 <keyword> site:openreview.net`.
