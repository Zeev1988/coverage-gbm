---
name: sprint-review
description: Use when reviewing a research sprint submission, project writeup, or draft paper for scope, methodological rigor, positioning against literature, and extension potential.
---

Read `docs/guides/SPRINT-REVIEW.TEMPLATE.md` and answer every guiding question for the submission. Cite specific passages, numbers, or figures. Don't give generic praise.

- **Rigor**: name the confounders and the sample-size or significance issues you find. When stats are missing, say what test would be needed.
- **Literature**: name the closest paper and state the gap concretely.
- Finish with the top 3 weaknesses ranked by severity, plus one concrete fix for each.

For calibration, you can look at real reviews: search `ICLR <year> <keyword> site:openreview.net`. `docs/guides/REVIEWING-ACADEMIC-WORK.md` works through one case. Common failure modes: overclaimed scope, equal-weighted averages of unequal criteria, unrepresentative prompts or settings, and "changed" being treated as "improved".

If you find a mistake that has shown up before (in the notebook, earlier reviews, or this conversation), *suggest* adding it to the "Mistakes we keep seeing" list in `docs/guides/EVAL-DESIGN-CHECKLIST.md`. Show the exact line and edit the guide only once the user approves.
