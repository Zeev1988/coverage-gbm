Read `docs/guides/MAKING-VIRAL-RESEARCH-GO-VIRAL.md` and apply the parts that fit the request.

**Design time** (Part 1):
- Names: 3 words or fewer, searchable, domain available (check it). Taxonomies have 3–8 categories with plain names.
- Phrasing ladder for each key concept: technical / industry / public / policy.
- Visuals: compare named, well-known players; use simple scoring; make the charts readable in grayscale.

**After completion** (Part 2):
- Media kit: 5–7 quotable lines; 3–5 captioned figures; a 1-page background; researcher bios.
- Talking points: headline finding / surprising discovery / concerning implication / call to action.
- Pitches: under 150 words, the key finding in the subject line, personalized for each outlet.

Never overstate results. Every claim must trace back to the paper. Interviews, follow-ups and sending anything stay with the humans. Draft, don't send.
