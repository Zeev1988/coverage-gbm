Read `docs/guides/ABSTRACT.v0.x.md` and apply it.

Nature's abstract anatomy (text version of `docs/guides/image.png`, which you can't see). Label each sentence with one of these:
1. **Context**: the broad setting
2. **Need**: *what we have*, then *what we want* (the gap)
3. **Task**: what we did
4. **Object of the document**: "This paper presents..."
5. **Findings**: concrete results, with numbers when there are any
6. **Conclusion**: the one-line takeaway
7. **Perspectives**: why it matters next

About 200 words at most, one paragraph (two if the venue allows).

When drafting: use situation → **complication** → *resolution*, then cut until no sentence can be removed.
When critiquing: label each sentence, then point out missing or bloated parts and claims the findings don't support.
When checking alignment: list the experiments or sections that don't support the abstract. Either cut them or change the abstract.
